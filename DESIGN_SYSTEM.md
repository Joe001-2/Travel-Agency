**Screen:** Express Consultancy – Homepage Template

---

## Brand & Style Philosophy

This design system establishes an aura of **executive authority, quiet luxury, and flawless reliability** for global corporate travel, VIP logistics, and international trade consultancy. Catering to C-suite executives, diplomatic delegates, mobility directors, and enterprise travel managers, the visual language strikes a balance between heritage prestige and modern operational agility.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Modern Corporate Heritage** with refined **Tactile Minimalism**. It avoids ostentatious gradients in favor of calibrated metallic accents, crisp structural grids, and deep architectural slate surfaces. Key emotional touchpoints are discretion, certainty, speed, and bespoke sophistication.

## Key Structure in `index.html`

1. **Navigation Header** (Fixed top, frosted glass blur, logo, nav links, 24/7 concierge contact, CTA)
2. **Hero Section** (Sovereign crest banner, headline, dual executive CTAs, trust badges, floating Tier 1 mobility badge)
3. **Metrics Bar** (99.4% visa clearance, 140+ countries, 25k+ travelers, 24/7 concierge)
4. **Interactive Search & Inquiry Desk** (Tabs for Visa Eligibility, Corporate Flights, and Concierge Dispatch)
5. **Core Services Grid** (Fast-track visa & immigration, corporate travel management, bespoke luxury expeditions, air ticketing desk)
6. **Prime Destinations Portfolio** (London & UK Tier visas, Dubai 10-Yr Golden Visa, Schengen Executive pass, etc.)
7. **Lead Consultation Form & Footer** (Direct booking, office hubs in Geneva, London, Dubai, Singapore)
